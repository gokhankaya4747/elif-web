Prayer times

  • Why do UK prayer timetables differ for Fajr and Isha?
    Why Fajr and Isha times differ between UK timetables and apps, what happens in summer, and why Hanafi Asr is later. How Elif calculates UK prayer times.
  • How are Isha and Fajr times set in Europe in summer?

Prayer

  • How do you make up missed prayers (qada)?
    Only the obligatory prayers and witr are made up, not the sunnahs. How to make the intention, when order matters and when not to pray. Based on Diyanet.
  • How do you pray when travelling (qasr)?
    Travelling 90 km+ and staying under 15 days makes you a traveller: four-rak'ah prayers become two. When it starts, sunnahs, combining, praying on transport.
  • How do you perform sajdah sahw (the prostration of forgetfulness)?
    Sajdah sahw for mistakes in prayer: when it is needed, how to do it step by step, doubts about rak'ahs, praying behind an imam and forgetting it. Diyanet-based.
  • How do you pray witr?
    Witr is three rak'ahs; in the third you say a takbir and recite the Qunut. Its ruling, its time, how to pray it and what to do if you forget the Qunut.
  • How many rak'ahs is Jumu'ah, and how is it prayed?
    Jumu'ah is 10 rak'ahs: 4 sunnah before, 2 fard, 4 sunnah after. Who must attend, the khutbah, what to do if you miss it, and zuhr al-akhir. Diyanet-based.

  • Who can receive zakat, and who cannot?
