What Elif includes

  • Prayer times — multiple calculation methods (Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, Diyanet for Türkiye), per-minute adjustment, a monthly timetable and a Ramadan imsakiye.
  • Adhan & notifications — a full adhan for every prayer, a gentle pre-prayer reminder, short chime tones for people who prefer a brief alert, and a silent option.
  • Lock screen countdown — a live countdown to the next prayer on the iOS lock screen, plus home and lock screen widgets.
  • The Qur'an — mushaf page view in Arabic, transliteration and multiple translations, verse-by-verse audio with reciter choice, memorisation mode, khatm tracking, search and bookmarks. All offline.
  • Qibla compass — precise direction, turn guidance, an AR camera mode and distance to Makkah.
  • Dhikr — a smooth digital tasbih, a step-by-step post-prayer tasbihat guide, salawat, morning and evening adhkar and a daily wird.
  • Duas & the 99 Names — categorised duas in Arabic with transliteration and translation, Asma al-Husna and 40 hadith.
  • Ask Elif — an AI assistant that answers in plain language and shows the source behind the answer, with madhhab selection.
  • Knowledge library — ilmihal, seerah, lives of the companions, an Islamic dictionary and quizzes.

Frequently asked questions

What is Elif?

Elif is an ad-free Islamic lifestyle app combining prayer times and adhan notifications, a qibla compass, the full Qur'an with recitation and multiple translations, a dhikr counter, duas, a knowledge library and a source-citing AI assistant in one app. It is free to download on iOS and Android and is fully localised in seven languages.

Is Elif free?

Yes. The download is free and everything needed for daily worship is free to use. An optional Elif Premium subscription unlocks extra features; no core worship feature sits behind a paywall.

Does Elif show ads?

No — no banners, no pop-ups, no interstitials.

Which prayer time calculation methods does Elif support?

Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA and others, plus times matching the Diyanet for Türkiye. Each prayer can be fine-tuned by the minute.

Does Elif have a lock screen prayer countdown?

Yes. On iOS a Live Activity shows a live countdown to the next prayer on the lock screen, and there are home and lock screen widgets for prayer times and the daily verse.

Can I read the Qur'an offline?

Yes. Mushaf reading, translations, duas and dhikr all work without an internet connection.

What does Elif do with my location and data?

Prayer time and qibla calculations run on the device; your location is not sent to a server, and user data is never sold or shared for advertising. Cloud backup is optional. See the privacy policy.

Which languages does Elif support?

English, Turkish, Arabic, French, Indonesian, Bengali and Urdu — with right-to-left support for Arabic and Urdu.

What is "Ask Elif"?

An AI assistant that answers religious questions in plain language and shows the verse, hadith or classical source the answer relies on. It follows the Ahl al-Sunnah tradition and lets you choose the Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki or Hanbali madhhab. It is not a fatwa authority — for binding rulings, consult a qualified scholar.
